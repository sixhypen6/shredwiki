1. Rotational Mechanics (2-Tick Cutter Cycling)

At the core of Demean is high-speed rotational positioning. The drive assembly spins the blade array fast enough to cycle individual cutter coordinates every 2 physics ticks (60Hz engine rate).

Mechanism:
By rotating the exact angular distance required to place a new cutter face into the identical spatial coordinates of the preceding cutter, the blade continuously forces collision frame updates across target hitboxes to achieve fluid phase passthrough.

2. Hyper Motor2 Stacking

To sustain 2-tick cycling under physical contact without torque stall, Demean setups utilize Hyper Motor2s—stacked Motor2 block configurations tuned to extreme torque limits (e.g., values scaling past 99,999 × 1,000,000).

This force overrides standard constraint resistance, allowing the blades to slice through assemblies regardless of enemy mass or material density.

3. Cutter Enhancement (CE) Synergy

While Demean Tech primarily governs phase clipping and noclip stability, it should ideally be paired with Cutter Enhancement (CE) for maximum damage output.

Running Demean rotational speeds on a tuned CE blade layout synchronizes tick-perfect collision refreshes directly with cutter hit cooldowns, yielding the highest possible hits-per-second while phasing through targets.

5. Optimal Vanilla Architecture

To maximize phasing fluidity without self-destructing or flinging the host build, legitimate vanilla Demean setups follow specific design principles:

  • Airy Construction: Keeping cutter density low to minimize physical drag and prevent unwanted kinetic feedback.
  • Dampened Blade Layering: Stacking multiple layers of dampened blades behind the Hyper Motor2 drive to absorb micro-shocks during phase passthrough.

6. The "Perfect Demean" Misconception

Engine Limitations & Exclusions:

True, absolute 100% zero-resistance "perfect demean" is mathematically unachievable within vanilla Plane Crazy engine limits. Builds claiming total, frictionless phase passthrough typically rely on third-party scripts or client injection, which are excluded from official wiki documentation.
